Why Nexus

Presence over Noise

No infinite feed. No algorithmic dopamine. You open Nexus to be present with specific people in specific contexts — or to rest in silence.

Circles of trust

You decide who sees what. Close, Work, Explore, Anon — different masks, different rules. No public profile by default.

Ephemeral by design

Most things disappear. Only what you explicitly Seal becomes permanent. Memory is a choice, not a default.

Attention states

“Open to deep talk”, “In focus”, “Quiet”, “Only urgent”. Others see your state. You protect your attention instead of performing availability.

You own the graph

Identity, relationships and history belong to you. Not to a platform that rents them back with ads.

This is a living prototype. The goal is evolution of communication, not another social network.
